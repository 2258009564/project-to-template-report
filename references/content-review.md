# Content review before Word insertion

Freeze a readable draft of structured blocks, then switch from authoring to a separate reviewer pass. Read afresh against user requirements, factual evidence, template topics and relevant local preferences. Do not treat author explanations or prior self-assessment as proof. This is a separate self-review, not a claim of genuinely independent blind peer review. Delegate only when authorized for the current task.

Check sentence clarity, missing subjects, ambiguous references, awkward expression and redundancy. Check paragraph logic, unsupported conclusions, contradictions, causal links, transitions and repetition. Check document-wide terminology, names, numbers, implemented/planned distinctions, section-topic alignment and requested coverage. Polish without changing facts or inventing support; preserve protected quotations and template labels.

Record concrete findings in the task-local QA ledger with passage, defect, repair and recheck result. Repair and reread changed passages in context; search all affected occurrences when facts or terminology change. Stop when no actionable content defect remains and required claims have evidence. Unknown facts stay explicitly unknown or blank. Keep private memory details out of shareable review records.

Only then insert reviewed prose into Word. Later prose changes require a review of affected content before reinsertion. Formatting-only tasks do not authorize rewriting. This content gate precedes and does not replace the existing rendered-page verification. Keep review drafts task-local unless requested as deliverables.
